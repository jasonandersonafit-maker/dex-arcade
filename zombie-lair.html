ZOMBIE LAIR

Stop the zombies before they reach you. Headshots kill in one hit. You have 5 lives. Each level, the zombies get faster, dodge more and start jumping. Every 5th level is a boss fight, and you unlock better guns as you go. Don't shoot the people yelling "HELP!" or you lose a heart.

On Quest: press Enter VR at the bottom. Pull a trigger to shoot (hold for rapid fire). Push a thumbstick left or right to slide. Press B for a missile.

On PC: left click to shoot where the mouse points (hold for rapid fire). A / D or the arrow keys slide you. Right click (or B) fires a missile at the mouse pointer.

Pause: P or Esc on PC, the X button on Quest.
